Sustainability: Myths vs Facts

Breaking down the most common misconceptions in the zero-waste world.

Myth

"Biodegradable" plastic is safe for the ocean.

Most biodegradable plastics only break down in industrial composting facilities at high temperatures, not in marine environments.

Fact

Refilling is 10x more efficient than recycling.

The energy required to collect, sort, and process plastic for recycling far outweighs the impact of simply reusing a glass or metal container.
